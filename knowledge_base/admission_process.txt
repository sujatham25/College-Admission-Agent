=== UNDERGRADUATE APPLICATION PROCESS ===

Step 1: Register on the Admission Portal
- Visit the official admissions portal at: https://admissions.democollege.edu (demo URL)
- Click "New Registration" and provide your name, email address, and mobile number.
- A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered mobile number.
- Verify the OTP to activate your account and receive your Application ID.

Step 2: Fill the Online Application Form
- Log in with your Application ID and password.
- Fill in personal details: name, date of birth, gender, category (General/SC/ST/OBC/EWS), address.
- Fill in academic details: 10+2 board, year of passing, subjects, marks obtained.
- Enter entrance exam details: JEE Main application number and percentile (for B.Tech).
- Select your preferred programme and up to three specialisation choices in order of preference.

Step 3: Upload Required Documents
- Upload scanned copies of all required documents (see the Required Documents section for the full list).
- Documents must be in PDF or JPG format; maximum file size: 2 MB per document.
- Ensure all documents are clearly legible before uploading.

Step 4: Pay the Application Fee
- Application fee: ₹1,000 for General/OBC candidates; ₹500 for SC/ST candidates.
- Payment can be made online via Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI.
- Save the payment receipt; the Application ID becomes active only after successful payment.

Step 5: Submit the Application
- Review all entered information carefully before submitting.
- Once submitted, the application form cannot be edited.
- Download and save the completed application form PDF for your records.

Step 6: Admit Card / Interview Call Letter
- Shortlisted candidates will receive an admit card or interview call letter via registered email and SMS.
- Admit cards for entrance-based programmes will be available for download from the portal 7 days before the test/interview date.

Step 7: Entrance Test / GD-PI (Programme-specific)
- B.Tech candidates: Admission based on JEE Main score; no separate college test.
- MBA candidates: Group Discussion (GD) followed by Personal Interview (PI) at the college campus.
- M.Sc Data Science: College Entrance Test (CET) at the campus.
- PG Diploma programmes: Personal interview (may be conducted online).

Step 8: Merit List Publication
- Provisional merit list will be published on the admissions portal.
- Students are allotted seats based on merit, category, and programme availability.
- Three rounds of counselling/allotment will be conducted until all seats are filled.

Step 9: Acceptance of Offer and Fee Payment
- Selected candidates must accept the offer by logging into the portal.
- Partial fee payment (seat booking amount) is required within 5 days of offer publication to confirm the seat.
- Failure to pay within the deadline will result in forfeiture of the allocated seat.

Step 10: Reporting and Document Verification
- Candidates must report to the college campus on the date specified in the offer letter.
- Bring all original documents for physical verification by the admissions committee.
- After successful verification, the admission process is complete and the student is formally enrolled.

=== POSTGRADUATE APPLICATION PROCESS ===

- The process follows the same 10 steps as above.
- Additional step for MBA: GD and PI are conducted after document screening.
- M.Tech candidates with valid GATE score proceed directly to document verification; no separate test.
- Sponsored candidates for M.Tech must submit a letter from their employer.

=== IMPORTANT NOTES ===

- Applications submitted after the deadline will not be considered under any circumstances.
- Incomplete applications (missing documents or unpaid fee) will be rejected without notice.
- Application fee is non-refundable.
- Candidates can track their application status on the admissions portal at any time.
- For technical issues with the portal, contact: admissions.support@democollege.edu or call the helpline at 1800-XXX-XXXX (demo number).

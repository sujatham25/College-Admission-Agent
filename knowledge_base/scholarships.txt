=== MERIT-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS ===

1. Chancellor's Merit Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Top 5 rank holders in the college entrance merit list for B.Tech CSE.
   - Benefit: 100% waiver on tuition fee for the first year; renewed annually if CGPA ≥ 9.0.
   - Number of awards: 5 per batch.

2. Academic Excellence Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Students who scored 95% or above in 10+2 (for UG programmes).
   - Benefit: 50% waiver on tuition fee for the first year.
   - Renewal: Maintained if CGPA ≥ 8.5 in the previous academic year.
   - Number of awards: 20 per batch across all UG programmes.

3. Dean's Scholarship (for Continuing Students)
   - Eligibility: Students who achieved CGPA ≥ 9.0 in the previous academic year.
   - Benefit: 25% waiver on tuition fee for the next academic year.
   - Applicable from 2nd year onwards.

=== NEED-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS ===

4. Financial Assistance Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Family annual income below ₹3,00,000; minimum 60% in 10+2 or CGPA ≥ 6.5.
   - Benefit: Up to ₹40,000 per year (varies based on financial need assessment).
   - Application: Submit income certificate (Form 16 or Income Tax Return) with the scholarship form.
   - Deadline: Apply within 30 days of admission.

5. Girl Child Education Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Female students from families with annual income below ₹5,00,000.
   - Benefit: ₹20,000 per year.
   - Renewal: Automatic if CGPA ≥ 6.0 maintained.

=== GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIPS (FACILITATED BY THE COLLEGE) ===

6. National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Scholarships
   - Includes: Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students from Central and State Government.
   - Eligibility: As per the respective government scheme; college facilitates the application.
   - Students should visit the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) for details.
   - College Scholarship Cell assists with the application process every July–September.

7. Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF)
   - Eligibility: Wards of CAPF/Railway Protection Force personnel.
   - Benefit: As per Government of India norms.
   - Apply through the Kendriya Sainik Board (KSB) portal.

=== SPORTS AND CULTURAL SCHOLARSHIPS ===

8. Sports Excellence Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Students who have represented the state or national level in recognised sports.
   - Benefit: 50% waiver on tuition fee.
   - Requirement: Must submit a certificate from the relevant sports association and continue active participation in college sports teams.

9. Cultural Talent Scholarship
   - Eligibility: Students with outstanding achievement in music, dance, drama, or fine arts at national level.
   - Benefit: ₹15,000 per year.
   - Selection: Audition/portfolio review by the Cultural Committee.

=== MINORITY AND SPECIAL CATEGORY SCHOLARSHIPS ===

10. Minority Community Scholarship
    - Eligibility: Students belonging to notified minority communities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) with family income below ₹6,00,000.
    - Benefit: ₹25,000 per year.
    - Apply through the college Minority Scholarship Cell.

11. Persons with Disability (PwD) Scholarship
    - Eligibility: Students with 40% or more disability (disability certificate required).
    - Benefit: 30% waiver on tuition fee plus ₹5,000 per year for assistive devices.

=== INDUSTRY-SPONSORED SCHOLARSHIPS ===

12. TechCorp Future Leaders Scholarship (Demo Sponsor)
    - Eligibility: B.Tech CSE students with JEE Main percentile ≥ 90 and family income below ₹8,00,000.
    - Benefit: ₹50,000 per year for all 4 years.
    - Selection: Written test and interview conducted by the sponsor company in November.
    - Number of awards: 3 per year.

=== HOW TO APPLY FOR SCHOLARSHIPS ===

- Scholarship application forms are available on the admissions portal under "Scholarships" section.
- Most scholarships require submission within 30 days of taking admission.
- Required documents for scholarship applications: fee receipts, marksheets, income/category certificates.
- The Scholarship Committee reviews applications and announces results within 45 days.
- For scholarship-related queries: scholarships@democollege.edu
- Scholarship Cell office hours: Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM.

=== IMPORTANT NOTES ===

- Only one institutional scholarship (college-offered) can be availed at a time; government scholarships may be availed in addition.
- Scholarship benefits are applied as a fee waiver or direct bank transfer (as specified per scheme).
- Misrepresentation of information in scholarship applications will lead to cancellation of scholarship and disciplinary action.

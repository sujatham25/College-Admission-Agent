=== HOSTEL APPLICATION DEADLINE ===

- Hostel application must be submitted along with the admission acceptance.
- Hostel allotment is on a first-come, first-served basis within category quotas.
- Last date for hostel application: 20 June 2025 (for July 2025 intake).

=== LATE APPLICATIONS ===

- Late applications are not accepted under any circumstances after the published deadline.
- No extension of deadlines will be granted for individual candidates.
- Candidates are advised to apply well before the deadline to avoid technical issues.

=== SPOT ADMISSIONS ===

- If seats remain vacant after all three rounds, spot admission rounds may be conducted.
- Spot admission dates will be announced on the college website and portal.
- Spot admissions are conducted at the college campus; candidates must be present in person.
- Indicative spot admission window (if applicable): 25 June 2025 – 28 June 2025 (UG).

=== KEY REMINDERS ===

- All deadlines are in Indian Standard Time (IST).
- The college is not responsible for last-minute failures due to technical issues on the candidate's end.

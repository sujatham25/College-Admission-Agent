=== DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSION ===

All B.Tech / B.Sc / B.Com / BBA Applicants Must Submit:

1. Class 10 (Secondary) Marksheet
   - Original plus two self-attested photocopies.
   - Required to verify date of birth and basic academic record.

2. Class 12 (Senior Secondary) Marksheet
   - Original plus two self-attested photocopies.
   - If result is pending, a provisional certificate from the school/board is acceptable for registration.
   - Final marksheet must be submitted at the time of document verification.

3. Class 12 (Senior Secondary) Pass Certificate / Migration Certificate
   - Original required for transfer from another state board.

4. Transfer Certificate (TC)
   - From the last school/college attended.

5. Character Certificate
   - Issued by the principal of the last attended institution.
   - Should not be older than 6 months from the date of application.

6. Category Certificate (if applicable)
   - SC / ST / OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate issued by a competent authority.
   - OBC certificate must be issued within 1 year of the application date.
   - EWS (Economically Weaker Section) income certificate from a competent authority.

7. Entrance Exam Scorecard (for B.Tech)
   - JEE Main scorecard showing the applicant's percentile and All India Rank (AIR).

8. Passport-size Photographs
   - Four recent colour photographs (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm), white background.

9. Photo Identity Proof (any one)
   - Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID / Driving Licence.

10. Address Proof (any one)
    - Aadhaar Card / Utility Bill (not older than 3 months) / Bank Passbook with address.

11. Medical Fitness Certificate
    - From a registered medical practitioner, confirming the candidate is physically fit for the programme.

=== ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS FOR POSTGRADUATE ADMISSION ===

12. Bachelor's Degree Certificate and All Semester/Year Marksheets
    - Original plus two self-attested photocopies.

13. Provisional Degree Certificate (if final degree not yet issued)
    - Acceptable at the time of application; final certificate required within 6 months of joining.

14. GATE / CAT / MAT / XAT / GMAT Scorecard (as applicable)
    - Original scorecard is mandatory for M.Tech (GATE) and MBA (CAT/MAT/XAT/GMAT).

15. Work Experience Certificate (for MBA sponsored candidates)
    - Experience letter on company letterhead specifying designation, period, and salary.

16. Employer Sponsorship Letter (for M.Tech sponsored candidates)
    - On official company letterhead, signed by the HR or authorised signatory.

=== DOCUMENTS FOR NRI / FOREIGN NATIONAL CANDIDATES ===

17. Passport (valid, with at least 1 year validity remaining)
18. Visa / OCI / PIO Card (as applicable)
19. Equivalency Certificate from Association of Indian Universities (AIU)
20. Bank statement showing sufficient funds for the duration of the programme

=== DOCUMENT SUBMISSION GUIDELINES ===

- All photocopies must be self-attested (signed by the candidate).
- Original documents are verified at the time of reporting to campus and returned after verification.
- Do not send originals by post; originals are checked in person only.
- Documents in a language other than English or Hindi must be accompanied by a notarised English translation.
- Failure to produce original documents at the time of verification will result in cancellation of admission.
- Keep a complete set of photocopies of all documents for your own records.

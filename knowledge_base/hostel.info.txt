HOSTEL AVAILABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

- Boys' Hostel:   3 blocks (Hostel A, Hostel B, Hostel C) — Total capacity: 600 students
- Girls' Hostel:  2 blocks (Hostel D, Hostel E) — Total capacity: 300 students
- Ph.D. Research Scholar Hostel: 1 block — Total capacity: 80 scholars (mixed gender, separate floors)

Total on-campus hostel capacity: approximately 980 students.
Hostel is available on a first-come, first-served basis for eligible students.

------------------------------------------------------------
ROOM TYPES AND HOSTEL FEE (Per Semester)
------------------------------------------------------------

BOYS' HOSTEL
- Triple Occupancy Room: INR 18,000 per semester (includes accommodation + electricity + basic furniture)
- Double Occupancy Room: INR 24,000 per semester
- Single Occupancy Room: INR 36,000 per semester (limited availability; priority to senior students)

GIRLS' HOSTEL
- Triple Occupancy Room: INR 18,000 per semester
- Double Occupancy Room: INR 24,000 per semester
- Single Occupancy Room: INR 36,000 per semester (priority to final-year students)

MESS CHARGES (Mandatory for hostel residents)
- Mess Fee:       INR 14,000 per semester (approximately INR 2,333 per month)
- Mess provides breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner.
- Special diet options (vegetarian/non-vegetarian) available.

ONE-TIME HOSTEL FEES
- Hostel Admission Fee:       INR 1,000 (non-refundable)
- Hostel Caution Deposit:     INR 5,000 (refundable at time of vacating)
- Bedding Charges (one-time): INR 2,000

------------------------------------------------------------
HOSTEL FACILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

- 24x7 Wi-Fi connectivity in all hostel blocks
- 24-hour security with CCTV surveillance
- Generator backup for uninterrupted power supply
- Purified drinking water (RO + UV) on every floor
- Common room with TV and recreational facilities
- Indoor games: Table tennis, carrom, chess
- Reading/study room in each hostel block
- Laundry facility (coin-operated washing machines)
- Medical room with a resident nurse (on-call doctor available)
- Housekeeping services (daily)
- Separate warden and assistant warden for each hostel

------------------------------------------------------------
HOSTEL RULES AND REGULATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Entry/exit timings: Boys' Hostels — 10:30 PM; Girls' Hostels — 9:00 PM (strict enforcement)
- Visitors are allowed only in the designated visitor area during permitted hours (10 AM – 6 PM).
- Cooking in rooms is strictly prohibited.
- Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or drugs is strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate expulsion from the hostel.
- Students must maintain cleanliness and discipline at all times.
- Ragging in any form is punishable under law and the institute's anti-ragging policy.
- Damage to hostel property will be charged to the student's account.

------------------------------------------------------------
HOSTEL ALLOTMENT PROCESS
------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Apply for hostel accommodation through the admission portal at the time of admission.
Step 2: Indicate your room-type preference (single/double/triple).
Step 3: Allotment is done based on availability and priority (merit, year of study, distance from hometown).
Step 4: Allotment letter is shared via email.
Step 5: Pay the hostel fee and caution deposit before the move-in date.
Step 6: Report to the Hostel Office (Admin Block, Ground Floor) for room key handover.
